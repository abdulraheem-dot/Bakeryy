We started with a borrowed oven and a belief: everyday bread should be extraordinary. Today, we still do it the slow way — with British-grown grains, a living sourdough starter and a 36-hour fermentation.

No improvers. No unnecessary ingredients. Just skilled hands, good grain and enough time to let the flavour find its way.
